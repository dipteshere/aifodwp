# Help Guide: Work screen stays blank or reconnects

Version: v1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

Your files and data are safe.

If your work screen stays blank after you log in, or you keep getting signed out and back in, try these steps in order:

1. Wait 30 to 60 seconds after you log in.
Expected result: Your work screen opens on its own.

2. If nothing changes, sign out fully from your work app or work website.
Expected result: You return to the login page.

3. Close the app or internet page, then open it again.
Expected result: A new login screen appears.

4. Log in one more time.
Expected result: You reach your normal work screen.

5. If the same issue happens again, stop trying and contact the service desk.
Expected result: The issue is handled quickly without repeated interruptions.

## What to include in your help request
Please copy these details into your support message:
- Your work account name
- The time the problem started
- The time of your latest login failure
- What you saw: blank screen, repeated sign-out and sign-in, or both
- Whether it cleared by itself after waiting
- Your computer name (if known)
- A screen picture, if possible

Contact the service desk if the screen is still blank after one more try, if you keep getting signed out, or if you cannot work normally.
