# My S: Drive Is Missing — What To Do

**Version:** v1.0 | **Date:** 07/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

---

## Don't worry — your files are safe

Your files have not been deleted or lost. The folder has simply not connected to your computer this session. Everything stored there is still on the secure company file server, untouched.

---

## What has happened?

When you signed in, your computer did not automatically connect to the shared **Finance (S:)** drive. This is a known issue that our IT team is aware of and working on. In the meantime, you can reconnect to the drive yourself by following the steps below — it only takes a minute.

---

## Try this first — reconnect the drive yourself

1. Open **File Explorer** (the folder icon on your taskbar).
2. In the left panel, click **This PC**.
3. At the top of the screen, click the **Computer** tab, then click **Map network drive**.
4. In the **Drive** box, select **S:**.
5. In the **Folder** box, type exactly:  `\\finbridge-fs01\Finance`
6. Tick the box that says **Reconnect at sign-in**.
7. Click **Finish**.
8. If asked, enter your normal work **username and password**.

The S: drive should now appear under **This PC** in File Explorer and you can access your files as normal.

---

## If the steps above did not work — try signing out and back in

1. Save any open work first.
2. Click the **Start** button → your name or profile picture → **Sign out**.
3. Sign back in and check whether the S: drive has appeared.

---

## Still not working? Contact the Service Desk

Please do not try anything else. Call or raise a ticket with the Service Desk and mention the following details so we can help you faster:

- **Your name and username**
- The **name of your computer** (shown at the bottom of your login screen, or ask a colleague to check it for you in Settings → About)
- The message you saw, if any (e.g. "Network name cannot be found")
- **Approximately what time** you signed in and noticed the drive was missing
- Whether the steps above worked, partially worked, or made no difference

We aim to have this resolved for you as quickly as possible. Thank you for your patience.
