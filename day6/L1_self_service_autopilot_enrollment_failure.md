# Help Guide: Device setup fails during company enrollment

Version: v1.0  
Date: 11/08/2026  
Status: Draft

Your files and account are safe.

If your new or reset Windows device shows an enrollment failure during setup, follow these steps in order:

1. Keep the device connected to a stable network (home, office, or mobile hotspot).
Expected result: Connection stays active with no disconnect pop-ups.

2. Restart the device once and retry setup.
Expected result: Setup resumes and asks for your work sign-in again.

3. Sign in with your normal work account only.
Expected result: Device continues to company setup screens.

4. If you see an enrollment error again, stop and contact the service desk.
Expected result: Support can fix the enrollment state without repeated failed attempts.

5. Do not try repeated resets on your own after a second failure.
Expected result: Device history is preserved for faster support resolution.

## What to include in your help request
Please copy these details into your support message:
- Your work account name
- Device name (if shown)
- Time of the latest failure
- Exact error code and message on screen
- Whether this device was used before with company management
- A photo of the error screen, if possible

Contact the service desk immediately if setup fails more than once, if you cannot reach the desktop, or if you see a code similar to 0x80180014.
