# Proactive Notice: AVD Sign-In and Account Lockout Issue

We are aware of a growing issue this morning where some colleagues cannot sign in to AVD or are seeing account lockouts. This is a known issue and our support and engineering teams are actively working on it now.

As an interim workaround, please use the same self-service steps:
1. Wait 30 to 60 seconds after login.
2. If nothing changes, sign out fully from the work app or website.
3. Close the app/browser, reopen it, and sign in one more time.
4. If it fails again, stop retrying and contact the Service Desk.

Please avoid repeated sign-in attempts, as this can increase lockout risk and slow recovery.

We will provide the next all-staff update by 11:30 AM today, even if we are still investigating.

Please stay calm and continue with alternative tasks where possible while we work this through. Thank you for your patience. We know this is disruptive, and we are treating it as a priority to restore stable access as quickly and safely as possible.
