# Executive Summary for Leadership

The Windows 11 migration is now in a much healthier place for most colleagues. Early access and file-availability pain points have largely settled, and day-to-day confidence is improving as more staff report stable sign-in, reliable remote access, and a smoother overall experience. The tone of feedback has shifted from urgent disruption to general satisfaction, with many teams now able to work normally and with fewer interruptions than in the first week.

Two issues still require focused attention. First, the floor 3 printing problem remains unresolved and is causing repeated workarounds and growing frustration in Finance. Second, we have a newer issue where large spreadsheet work is unstable for some users, which is now affecting productivity in critical finance tasks. In parallel, we are actively validating a recent policy change linked to slower morning start-up experience in one business group and are using controlled checks to isolate the exact contributor before any wider rollback. We are treating these as priority service risks, giving regular updates, and balancing speed with safe change control to avoid creating new disruption.
