### 1) AVD sign-in and account lockout failures (count: 7)

### 2) Missing or unsafe files after migration (OneDrive/Desktop) (count: 5)

One sentence for manager: "The second priority is missing OneDrive/Desktop files across 5 reports, as users can log in but cannot complete critical deliverables without their data."

### 3) Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance) (count: 3)
Why it ranks here: Despite fewer comments, this remains a Blocker because affected users are explicitly blocked from month-end finance work, so impact outweighs higher-count minor/friction themes.
One sentence for manager: "Third priority is S:/Finance shared-drive denial (3 reports), which is a lower-volume but high-impact blocker to month-end reporting tasks."
