If you cannot sign in or your sign-in is taking much longer than normal, use these steps.

1. Restart your device once and try again.
Expected result: Temporary sign-in delays may clear after restart.

2. Make sure you are connected to the company network or VPN.
Expected result: Your device can reach the services needed for sign-in.

3. Wait a few minutes before retrying multiple sign-ins.
Expected result: You avoid repeated failed attempts while IT is restoring service.

4. Contact support if the problem continues.
Provide:
- your device name
- when the issue started
- whether you see a password error or only a long delay

What IT will do:
- confirm whether other Floor 6 users are affected
- check recent update and app rollout timing
- collect sign-in and device evidence
- roll back the recent change if confirmed necessary
