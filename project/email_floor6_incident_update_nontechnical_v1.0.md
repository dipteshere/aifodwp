Subject: Floor 6 service update

Hello everyone,

Here is a clear update on the issues reported on Floor 6.

What happened
On Friday afternoon, a new work app was rolled out. After that, several people on Floor 6 reported problems. Some could not sign in, some had very slow sign-in, and at least one person reported missing desktop shortcuts. We also received one report that Copilot showed a client matter the person believes they should not see.

What is being done now
We have paused the rollout to affected devices. We are removing impacted devices from that rollout group and applying a rollback for those users. We are checking each step to make sure sign-in and desktop behavior return to normal.

For the Copilot concern, we are treating it as a priority and reviewing it with our security and compliance teams. We are preserving the details needed to confirm exactly what was shown and whether access was correct.

What is still open
We are still confirming the exact root cause and full user impact count. We are also still validating the Copilot report before making any final conclusion.

What you can expect next
You will continue to get regular updates in plain language. If we need action from you, we will send simple instructions. We will share a recovery time only when we can do so accurately.

Thank you for your patience while we complete safe recovery and verification.
