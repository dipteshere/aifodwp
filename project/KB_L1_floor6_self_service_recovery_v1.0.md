A recent app rollout may cause slow login, login failure, or missing shortcuts for some Floor 6 users.

What you can do now:
1. Restart your device once and try sign-in again.
2. If sign-in works but desktop looks different, open apps from Start Search while fixes complete.
3. Keep your device connected to company network/VPN for policy sync.
4. Wait for the service update from IT before retrying multiple times.

What IT is doing:
1. Removing affected devices from the rollout group.
2. Rolling back the app change on impacted devices.
3. Checking login and desktop stability before confirming recovery.

When to contact support immediately:
1. You still cannot sign in after one restart.
2. You see repeated password failures.
3. You believe Copilot showed a restricted matter.

Provide to support:
- Your device name
- Time issue started
- Error message screenshot (if available)
